    Naga button setup

    Your Naga side buttons send whatever keys Razer Synapse assigns. Click Learn, then press that button so the simulator knows which button is which.

    Tip: in Synapse, set the 6 side buttons to Numpad 1–6. WoW can bind numpad keys separately from the number row, so they won't clash with 1–6 on your keyboard.
